# Directory Structure: Backend/training

Model training and evaluation scripts for word-level and sentence-level sign recognition.

All the word-level trainers share the **same data selection rule** (implemented in
`Backend/utils/dataloader_word.py`): only the top-most frequent classes of the word
corpus are loaded — a class with fewer than **14** clips is eliminated from the loader
(its files stay on disk, the pretrainer still uses them) and every surviving class
contributes at most **15** clips. The resulting vocabulary (228 classes) is published in
`Backend/models/word_vocabulary/classes.json` and used by every trainer, every
evaluation script and the live inference path.

All the geometric/temporal augmentations live in `Backend/utils/pose_augmentation.py`
(mirror around the signer's own axis, tilt around the neck, bounded left/right shift,
speed up / slow down, temporal shift, SpecAugment, temporal hole filling).

## Files
- `evaluate_reconformer.py`: Generic evaluation script for ReConformer model architectures.
- `evaluate_synthetic_sentences.py`: Calculates Word Error Rate (WER) and loss metrics on synthetic sentence data.
- `generate_training_plot.py`: Visualizes training and validation curves from metric histories.
- `phase2_trainer.py`: Phase 2 fine-tuning script — loads the pretrained Transformer+TCNN backbone into the ISLSentenceReconformer (BiGRU→Transformer→TCNN) for word-level classification with curriculum FPS (1→50 fps, the source rate is NOT a cap). Loads the corpus through the shared selection rule and augments with the canonical set. Saves to `models/pretrained_phase_2_model/`.
- `phase3_trainer.py`: Phase 3 word-level fine-tuning from Phase-2 checkpoint. Replaces head for the new class count (the mismatching head is dropped by `utils/checkpoint_utils.py`), freezes backbone for 5 epochs then unfreezes with differential LR, curriculum FPS (the source rate is not a cap), augmentation after split. Saves to `models/phase3_word_model/`.
- `phase4_trainer.py`: Phase 4 full-FPS fine-tuning from Phase-3 checkpoint. No frame clipping. Augmentation: random time-stretch, mirror, tilt, bounded shift (applied after train/val split). 50 epochs: transformer frozen for first 30 (CosineAnnealingWarmRestarts on RNN+CNN+head), then all layers train for final 20 (CosineAnnealing). Saves to `models/phase4_word_model/`.
- `phase5_trainer.py`: Phase 5 maximum accuracy squeeze reusing exact Phase 4 split (loaded from `split_paths.json`, which must be regenerated with `scripts/save_phase4_split.py` after a vocabulary change). All layers unfrozen, OneCycleLR with SWA. Saves to `models/phase5_word_model/`.
- `phase6_trainer.py`: Phase 6 high-precision squeeze trainer reusing exact Phase 4/5 split. Lookahead wrapping decoupled AdamW, Annealed LR combined with ReduceLROnPlateau, Model EMA, SpecAugment temporal frame masking, and gentle mixup. Saves to `models/phase6_word_model/`.
- `pretrainer.py`: Transformer + Temporal CNN (TCNN) pretraining script training on MediaPipe pose sequences with median length normalization and geometric augmentations. Trains on the WORD corpus with a real file-level split (2643 train / 684 val clips, no overlap) — it used to load ONE clip per class and use it for both partitions, so its "98% validation accuracy" was memorisation. Cosine with warm restarts + a plateau escape.
- `structure.md`: Directory structure documentation for training.
- `train_conformer_ctc.py`: **Shared foundation for every CTC trainer.** ISL Conformer-CTC (MHA + Depthwise Conv, no global pooling) with a 4-stage curriculum (single-word, 2-word, N-word progressive, unfrozen polish). Also hosts the pieces reused by the real-sentence trainers: `AntiCollapseCTCLoss` (log-prob floor so "emit nothing" is not free + emission barrier + blank cap + spike penalty), `MaskedBatchNorm1d` (padding-aware BatchNorm), `harmonize_features()` (re-anchors the three differently-normalised corpora into one frame), `match_pretraining_domain()` (rewrites a clip into the isolated-word geometry the backbone was pretrained on), `escape_local_minimum()` (perturbation + BN reset + CTC-head rebalance + LR restart), and the augmentation set. The vocabulary is the published one and the Phase-6 word classifier is transferred INTO THE CTC HEAD BY CLASS NAME (the row order of the old checkpoint does not match anymore). **The student-teacher mode** (`--kd_weight`) adds the consistency loss against the frozen word teacher on the words the teacher gets right, which is the dense signal the CTC needs to escape the blank collapse. `--stride` adds a strided convolution front-end (1/2/4) in front of the self-attention, `--gru_expand` chooses where the recurrent expansion happens (`before` = the Transformer receives the expanded tensors, `after` = the Conformer-CTC design), `--causal` makes the whole stack causal for streaming, `--layer_lrs` sets one learning rate per LAYER and `--freeze_transformer_epochs` / `--freeze_backbone_epochs` stage the release of the pretrained layers. `--beam_width` switches the evaluation to the prefix beam search, optionally rescored by the word-level language model (`--beam_alpha`). **NOTE: the current version cannot train stage 1 (0% exact match, reproduced with both the HEAD and the f4ed0ce revision on 2026-10-20) — the saved `models/isl_conformer_ctc/stage{1,2,3}_*.pth` are good (67.2% / 57.8% exact match) and should be kept as the warm start.**
- `train_real_sentences.py`: Real continuous human sign sentence fine-tuner training Conformer-CTC on real continuous video streams with selective in-scope relabeling. Applies the cross-corpus feature harmonisation, the anti-collapse emission barrier, the padding-aware backbone, the blank-collapse watchdog, the local-minimum escape and an SWA tail checkpoint. The sentence targets are re-indexed onto the current vocabulary BY NAME. Default `--max_out_scope 5` (1136 clips / 692 sentences) instead of the old `1` (220 clips / 36 sentences) that made the model memorise the train split. `--domain_match 1` additionally rewrites each clip into the isolated-word geometry the backbone was pretrained on (arm extended, hand 1.46x larger). `--mix 1` draws the training set at random from three sources — isolated word clips (sign inventory), synthetic sentences (CTC alignment, padded with REAL continuous frames) and the real sentences (target domain). At startup it prints the **label prior of every source and of the mixture** (see `utils/word_distribution.py`): the two synthetic sources draw their classes uniformly, the continuous corpus is dominated by six pronouns, and the mixture lifts the effective support of the prior from 23 to 175 of the 228 classes. Both prior-correction knobs default to OFF on purpose, with the measurements: `--mix_word_cap 60` gives 87.2% WER (it DROPS ~40% of the only real data) and `--class_balance_alpha 0.5` gives 98.9% (fixing the prior in the loss breaks the CTC alignment) versus 75.0% with both off.
- `train_real_sentences_phase2.py`: Phase 2 continuous sentence fine-tuner incorporating CosineAnnealingWarmRestarts, SpecAugment temporal cutout, and a class-balanced focal CTC loss built on the shared anti-collapse loss. Saves to `models/isl_real_sentences_phase2/`.
- `train_real_final.py`: Master regularized continuous trainer warm-starting from Phase 2 real-sentence weights with the anti-collapse emission barrier, SpecAugment temporal cutout, zero-leakage prompt splits and the local-minimum escape. Saves to `models/isl_real_final/`.
- `train_reconformer_word.py`: Primary training script for ReConformer word-level models. Uses the shared word dataloader (top-most frequent classes, 15-clip budget, `[NOTHING]` idle class) and the canonical augmentation set.
- `train_synthetic_sentences.py`: Phase 1 continuous sentence trainer using CTC loss. Generates synthetic sentences (1-10 words, no repetition, smooth co-articulation) from the selected word clips, and trains the CTC head over a frozen Phase 6 backbone.
- `train_synthetic_sentences_phase2.py`: Phase 2+ unfrozen sentence trainer with dynamic phase auto-detection and cycling (automatically detects latest Phase K, initializes from its weights, and trains Phase K+1 into `islsentence_synthetic_phase{K+1}/`). Implements discriminative learning rates, Model EMA, Cosine Annealing, and regularized CTC loss.
